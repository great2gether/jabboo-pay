Jabboo Pay

PayPal Sandbox · test money only

PayPal for everyone who can’t easily type or see. Say who, how much and what for – Jabboo Pay reads it back, waits for your “yes”, and creates the PayPal invoice or payment link.

Dictate with Jabboo and end with “Jabboo” – that presses Enter. Then say “yes” / “ja” to create it, “cancel” / “abbrechen”, or “repeat” / “wiederholen”. Ask “Has Max paid?” or “What is still open?”. Keyboard: Enter sends, Shift+Enter new line, Esc cancels.

Take or choose a photo of a note, order or business card. Text is recognised on this Mac (Apple Vision), then read back to you for a “yes”.

    Due dates of open invoices and payment links (built with Bryntum Calendar, trial loaded from jsDelivr). Ask “Was wird diese Woche fällig?”, “What is due next week?” or “Was ist überfällig?”.       Every step is recorded on this Mac: what was heard, what was read back, how it was confirmed (voice, key, button, MCP approval) and what PayPal answered. Each line carries the SHA-256 of the previous one, so any later change is detected. Say “Lies mir das Protokoll von heute vor” or “Prüfe das Protokoll”. Stored in data/audit.jsonl; turn off with JABBOO_PAY_AUDIT=0.

      Privacy: your voice is recognised on your Mac by Jabboo (NVIDIA Parakeet, offline). Only the structured request – recipient, amount, purpose – goes to the PayPal Sandbox. Nothing is created without your “yes”. Jabboo Pay never sends money.
